## Deviations / surprises

### Deviation 1 — Catalog size overshoots the RESEARCH.md §Q6 estimate

RESEARCH.md §Q6 predicted "5–15k canonical skills after dedup" (Decision F
in CONTEXT.md repeats the estimate). The actual local run produced
**23,056 admitted records** before dedup, with only 38 duplicates flagged
due to stale vectors (see Deviation 3).

**Root cause:** the 33,000-record raw dataset's slop-rejection rate at
`MIN_BODY_LENGTH=200` (was 500) is more permissive than research assumed.
After the slop-only filter + language-variant dedup, 23,056 survived (vs
the predicted ~30,458 → 5-15k after dedup). The 5-15k estimate assumed
30-50% of mega-repo content would dedup out — which would require fresh
vectors for ALL 23,056 records.

**Phase 3.1 stance:** ship as-is. The "comprehensive index + curation
surface" promise of the Wirecutter pivot (PROJECT.md → Core Value) is
honored by the dedup INFRASTRUCTURE; the dedup VOLUME on first ship is
gated by embedding budget. CI's `Embed skills (OpenAI)` step will
embed the deltas (>20k new records over the next few runs at the
embed-skills.js delta-only budget), then enrich.js's dedup effectiveness
catches up to the research estimate over ~5–10 daily runs.

### Deviation 2 — Featured tier ballooned to 56% (was 26%)

RESEARCH.md §Q6 predicted Featured tier to "stay roughly 500" because
score formula doesn't reward 0-star records highly. **Local run shows
12,980 Featured (56%)** — far over the §Q6 prediction.

**Root cause:** the score formula's 7-signal composite has 80% non-stars
weight (recency, frontmatter, docs, issues, license, description). With
MIN_STARS=10 gone, 0-star records that have good frontmatter + recent
push_at + permissive license score 90+ trivially. Stars at the 20%
weight no longer act as the implicit gate.

**Decision F is the load-bearing mitigation:** dedup hides ~30–50% of
records from default browse. But dedup only fires for the 1,078 records
that have vectors — see Deviation 3.

**Recommended Phase 3.1.x follow-up (NOT a 3.1 blocker):** recalibrate
the score formula's tier thresholds OR re-weight signals so Featured
maintains its "Wirecutter pick rarity." Phase 3.6's "Featured → Top"
rename is the natural moment.

### Deviation 3 — Vector coverage is 4.7% (1,078 of 23,056) — FLAG 4

This is the documented FLAG 4 caveat: vectors in
`data/skill-vectors.ndjson` are last-CI-snapshot (1,078 records), but
today's filter output is 23,056 records. **The audit's duplicate count
(38) UNDERESTIMATES actual duplicates by ~95%.** The real numbers come
from the CI run (Task 10), where the Embed step generates fresh vectors
for the full catalog before enrich.js runs.

This audit is therefore **a smoke-level proof that enrich.js wires up
end-to-end** and finds REAL duplicates in the vector subset — NOT a
calibrated dedup measurement.

### Deviation 4 — Slug collision count: 402 (research said 13)

The 13 collisions from RESEARCH.md §Q4 were measured against the 1,890-
record catalog. The 21x catalog growth predictably grew the collision
set; most new collisions are intra-org megarepo duplication
(`majiayu000/claude-skill-registry/*` alone contributes >300 entries).

**Stance:** the slug fix scales correctly. 402 redirect entries is well
under the "redirect list grows past ~50" trigger for KV migration
(CONTEXT.md R3). The hardcoded-in-worker approach (Task 5) handles
402 entries trivially (~30 KB JSON; well under the 2 MB Workers free
tier limit).

## Post-CI dry-run addendum (2026-05-26)

### CI dry-run #1 (run 26432610715) — found bug

- Build failed at `generate-badges.js`: ENOENT for 3-segment path-aware slug `microsoft/skills/azure-aigateway.svg`. The badge writer assumed 2-segment slugs (`owner/name`).
- Fix in `ddd3d5d`: ensureDir(dirname(target)) before each writeFileSync.

### CI dry-run #2 (run 26451472370) — found tier-budget cliff

- Build advanced through `generate-badges` ✓ → `compute-similar` ✓ (71 min, O(n²) at 33k) → `marketplace` ✓ → `mine-apis` ✓
- **Hard-failed at `check-tier-budget.js`**: 28,582 renderable pages (Featured+Solid) > 18,000 budget = blows the Cloudflare Workers Static Assets 20k free-tier cap.
- Root cause: the absolute-threshold scoring (Featured ≥ 90, Solid ≥ 70) was calibrated against the 1,885-record pre-3.1 catalog. At 33,944 records, the same thresholds promote 84% to renderable tiers.

### Resolution — percentile-based tier assignment

The "Featured ballooning" calibration debt (Deviation 2 above) was treated as a Phase 3.6 cosmetic concern. The dry-run revealed it's load-bearing for the $12/yr static-asset budget. Cannot be deferred.

Fix lands in this branch:
- `scripts/filter.js`: tier assignment moves from absolute thresholds to percentile rank (top 10% → Featured, next 30% → Solid, rest → Listed). Defense-in-depth `RENDERABLE_CAP=18000` trims Solid first if percentiles would overflow Cloudflare's budget (activates above ~45k catalog).
- `CLAUDE.md`: tier semantics updated to percentile-based.
- Expected post-fix tier counts at 33,944 catalog: Featured ~3,394 (top 10%), Solid ~10,183 (next 30%), Listed ~20,367 (rest). Renderable = 13,577 — well under the 18,000 budget with ~25% headroom.

### Calibration debt now closed (not just deferred)

The Phase 3.6 "Featured → Top" rename is now the ONLY remaining tier-related work. The percentile model makes "Featured" mean "top 10% of catalog" — a real signal, not an inflation-prone label.

## Projection vs. ceiling

- **Cold day** (first run on a branch or on main; GHA caches are branch-scoped): harvest 11 min + filter < 1 min + live embed ≈ 2 min + enrich ≈ 1 min + upload ≈ 1 min + cache restore/save ≈ 1 min ≈ **+16 min**.
- **Warm day:** harvest < 1 min + filter < 1 min + embed (deltas only) < 1 min + enrich ≈ 1 min + upload ≈ 1 min ≈ **+4 min**.
- Against the steady-state daily cron of ~2.5 h (150 min; STATE.md), the projected totals are ~166 min cold / ~154 min warm vs. the **330**-minute `timeout-minutes` ceiling: **a margin of ≥ 164 minutes** even on a cold day. The APIs.guru directory is frozen (2023 snapshot), so after day 1 its spec pass is effectively free.

The flip itself: change the job-env fallback `|| 'false'` → `|| 'true'` in `.github/workflows/daily-scrape.yml` and update the regex in `scripts/__tests__/workflow-flags.test.js` test 1, as one small reviewed commit. ## Known operational notes

- data.gouv.fr sends neither ETag nor Last-Modified, so its 7 pages are re-downloaded every run (≈ 5–7 s fixed).
- APIs.guru `list.json` and the two raw GitHub files honour `If-None-Match` → warm runs are mostly 304s. The sidecar (`data/feeds-etag-cache.ndjson`) and the raw corpus share the `apis-raw-ndjson-*` cache; api vectors use `api-vectors-*`.
- 16 specs exceed the 5 MB streamed cap (the GitHub family ×8, Microsoft Graph ×2, EC2, Zoom, Zuora, …). They keep `spec_url` but no spec-derived base URL or auth.
- Two harvester bugs surfaced by the real run, both fixed before these numbers were taken:
  1. Raw keying by `source:source_id` collapsed 42 records: 29 UK HMRC APIs share one URL, and 13 public-apis names repeat across categories. Fix 93e8cb2: the key now includes `content_sha`.
  2. Reused specs paid the per-host pool spacing. Fix aa50ea3.
